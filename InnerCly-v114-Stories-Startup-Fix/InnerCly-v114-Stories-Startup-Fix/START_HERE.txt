INNERCLY v114 — START HERE

1. Extract this ZIP into a new folder.
2. Double-click START_INNERCLY.bat.
3. Keep the black Command Prompt window open while using InnerCly.
4. Sign in with your existing account. The configured Owner username is @innercly_owner by default unless your setup already configures another owner.

Do not open index.html directly; the app needs the local server and database.
Do not open files inside the backups folder; v40 and v80 are preserved master backups, not launch files.
